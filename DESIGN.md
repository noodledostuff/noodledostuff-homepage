# Rooftop portfolio

## Composition

The rigged operator occupies the left side of the shot. A creator badge, location strip and resource shortcuts frame the skyline. Five staggered hexagonal buttons select cinematography and photography, editing and VFX, real-time 3D, motion capture, or live events. Each equips the character and reveals scene props.

On the right, the featured banner opens work related to the active discipline. The default is the LED ICVFX case study. The large cyan 作品 button opens selected work. The four narrow tiles below it lead to the skill tree, cameras, profile and contact. Chinese display lettering and English captions echo the reference HUD. Give every button an accessible name in the visitor’s selected language.

Keep the operator name and shot selector together at lower left. The eye and still camera sit opposite them, above the film archive shortcut and Hong Kong clock. On portrait screens, place the operation tiles below the face and keep the shot selector within reach. Open records in a right-side panel on desktop and a scrollable bottom sheet on phones.

## Color and motion

Use dark navy controls, cyan navigation, a raspberry-pink active shot and warm-white text. Use crisp angled silhouettes for navigation and rectangular surfaces for reading. Nearby tenements layer recessed windows, balconies and sign brackets against the distant skyline. Blue curtain-wall towers and illuminated rooftop signage rise over the street blocks. Baked city lighting gives the tanks and pipework glossy highlights. Standing water on the roof reflects the skyline from the current camera. Warm practical lights, depth haze and fine grain, scan lines and slight edge color separation carry through the shot. Apply the film finish to the scene and exported photos. Keep HUD lettering sharp.

The opening orbit lasts two minutes. Use slow changes in breathing and posture between shots. Reduced-motion visitors start with a still frame and can select any camera or resume motion in Settings.
The skill map connects the selected discipline to technique groups and individual tools. Selecting a tool shows its portfolio description below the map.
