# Main street portfolio

## Composition

Five discipline shortcuts frame the operator at a rain-soaked crossing among Mong Kok storefronts. Selecting one opens the operator’s dossier on the right. The lobby keeps its left-hand discipline shortcuts beside the work panel. Compact screens use a readable dialog.

Snowbreak’s main menu and operative screen guide the composition. The character sits just left of centre. Selected work anchors a vertical command group on the right, followed by the skill tree and contact details with downloads. On compact portrait screens the commands move below the character.

## Color and motion

Lobby commands use the game's mission and utility frames. The street, operator and equipment share the same view throughout. A dark record panel opens on the right. The native entrance runs into the standing idle. The camera eases from its current position into a slowly moving discipline-specific composition. Technique frames the hands and face. Equipment frames the selected production setup. Props appear at full size through a fine cyan-edged material dissolve. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see the finished state immediately. The city lights, actor and production gear reflect in the wet asphalt. The 72-second automatic camera sequence and subtle CRT texture stay behind the HUD.

The skill map connects disciplines, technique groups and individual tools. Selecting a tool reveals its portfolio description next to the operator. Level up advances its lobby level and draws the camera into an arc before returning to the selected framing.
